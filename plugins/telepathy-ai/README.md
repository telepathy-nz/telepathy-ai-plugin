# Telepathy AI

Telepathy AI is your company's business assistant. It keeps the picture of your work: projects, decisions, commitments and who is responsible for what.

## Use it

Start a conversation and Claude checks in with Telepathy: anything waiting for you, your top tasks and any question Telepathy has for you. As you work, mention what you're working on, a to-do, a decision or something to delegate, and Claude passes it on. Ask about a project, a decision or another team, and Telepathy answers from what you're allowed to see.

## Data

The plugin's connector sends Telepathy only what you say to Claude while you use it: check-ins, notes you ask it to keep, answers to its questions and the projects you open. It has no access to your email or files. Telepathy stores it in your company's own space, encrypted with a key that belongs to your company alone. See the data note Telepathy gave you for the full detail.
